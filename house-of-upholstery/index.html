What the shop takes on

Categories as the business presents them on its own site.

  1. 01

    Upholstery & reupholstery

    Sofas, armchairs, dining chairs, headboards, antiques and one-off pieces — rebuilt and recovered rather than replaced.

  2. 02

    Fitted slipcovers

    Made-to-measure slipcovers cut for the piece, for furniture that needs a fresh surface rather than a full rebuild.

  3. 03

    Foam, cushions & pillows

    Replacement foam and refilled or remade cushions and pillows, cut to size for home and commercial seating.

  4. 04

    Furniture & upholstery repair

    Repair work on frames, seats and covers — the structural side of keeping a good piece in service.

  5. 05

    Commercial seating

    Restaurant and booth seating, medical and dental chairs, office seating and other contract upholstery. See the commercial work.

  6. 06

    Sets & props

    Upholstery for movie sets and props, where a piece has to read correctly on camera.

More detail on how a job is discussed

The business states that estimates are done on location across the GTA — call to confirm and to talk through the piece. Bring a photo or a description of the furniture and what you want changed: cover, foam, cushions, repairs or a slipcover.
